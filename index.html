01 — Encryption

Encrypted on your device. Readable only by your team.

Most team chat is encrypted in transit — the server can still read everything. ClinikChat encrypts each message on the sender's device, and only your team's devices hold the keys.

  1. 01

    Keys live on your devices

    Each team member's device generates its own keypair. Private keys never leave the device — they're never uploaded, emailed, or recoverable by us.

  2. 02

    Messages are sealed before sending

    Every message is encrypted on the sender's device for the intended recipients before it touches the network.

  3. 03

    The server only ever sees ciphertext

    Our infrastructure routes and stores encrypted payloads. There is nothing readable on the server to leak, subpoena, or browse.

  4. 04

    Only your team's devices can open them

    Decryption happens locally, on the recipient's device. If you're not in the conversation, the message is mathematically out of reach.

“Even Praxentis can't decrypt your team's chat.”

03 — Australian by design

Patient data never leaves the country.

A clinic's internal conversation is operational data. ClinikChat keeps it on Australian soil, under Australian law, as a matter of architecture — not policy promises.

Application

Fly.io — Sydney region

The ClinikChat API and realtime service run in Fly.io's Sydney region, keeping traffic and storage inside Australia.

Storage

AWS — ap-southeast-2

Encrypted attachments and backups live in AWS Sydney (ap-southeast-2). No offshore replication, no foreign regions.

Legal posture

Privacy Act 1988 (Cth)

Built and operated in alignment with the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles — a brand feature, not a footnote.
